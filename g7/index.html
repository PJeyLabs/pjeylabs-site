About G Point 7

A local distributor that grew into a national supplier

Founded in Barnsley in 2011, G Point 7 markets and distributes quality alcohol, food and soft drinks of Eastern European origin across the United Kingdom. What began as a regional distributor in the north of England now serves trade customers nationwide.

We work the way our customers do — competitive pricing, reliable stock and a team that knows the products. Cash and carry at the warehouse, or delivered on our own vans.
